# Credits

People who have helped make PocketTS. Thank you!

## Code and ideas

- **[Stefan3Dx](https://github.com/Stefan3Dx)**: muting during phone calls, pausing a screen share for privacy, the
  notification's buttons and opening TeamSpeak links, first made in
  [his fork](https://github.com/Stefan3Dx/pocketts/commit/7d11bea19aa38d032cb50f97bca9e5ddf0aa4f58).
- **[chochrunius](https://github.com/chochrunius)**: found that a pasted `ts3server://` link lost its channel and
  passwords, and that connecting again after raising the security level lost a link's channel and privilege key, fixed
  in [their fork](https://github.com/chochrunius/pocketts/commit/9ca66a224c3d6e283689d826f660c40533d61b4e). Their fork
  also had the idea of a channel to join on each server, which became the favourites' channel.

## Testers

Everyone in the Google Play closed test, and everyone who reports problems and ideas in the
[issues](https://github.com/KorppuJauho/pocketts/issues).

Contributions are welcome: open an issue or a pull request, or share an idea in the
[discussions](https://github.com/KorppuJauho/pocketts/discussions). Translation corrections are welcome too (see
[TRANSLATIONS.md](TRANSLATIONS.md)).
